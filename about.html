The founder story

From enterprise operations to St. Louis businesses.

Joshua Hancock brings over a decade of experience supporting and leading technology in environments where reliability, speed, and operational continuity are essential.

His work has included complex facilities, multi-site IT operations, network infrastructure, technical team leadership, vendor coordination, project delivery, and preparation for operational disruption.

That experience made one thing clear: good IT is not simply fixing devices. It is understanding the operation, setting priorities, documenting systems, communicating clearly, and building technology that can be trusted when the business needs it most.

Why High Tech STL exists

Smaller businesses deserve serious technology leadership.

Many local companies have reached a point where technology is central to nearly every part of the operation, but a full internal IT department still does not make financial sense.

High Tech STL closes that gap. We give owners and leaders a trusted point of accountability, responsive help for their teams, and a practical path from recurring technology problems to a more reliable operation.
