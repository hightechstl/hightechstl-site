The Stone Listener

Role Sheet — Keep private from the other player.

Role Summary

You hear whispers in old stone. You came to Marrow Hill because buried places speak truer than fearful villages. You believe that understanding the shrine matters as much as escaping it.

Private Goal

Learn why the shrine was sealed, even if the truth makes restoration dangerous.

Starting Prompt

You hear whispers through old stone. The hill is not empty; it is waiting to be asked the right question.

What You Tend to Notice

  • History, motives, vows, and forgotten bargains
  • The moral cost behind practical choices
  • Whether a solution tells the truth about the past

Ask Yourself During Play

  • Is protection worth an old injustice?
  • What truth must the village hear?
  • Can mercy and safety coexist?
