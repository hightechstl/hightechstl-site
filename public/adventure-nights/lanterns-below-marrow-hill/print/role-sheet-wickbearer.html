The Wickbearer

Role Sheet — Keep private from the other player.

Role Summary

You carry the last ember from the village hearth. It reacts to truth, warmth, and old promises. Your family once helped hide the hill lantern, and you feel responsible for what happens next.

Private Goal

Restore the village lantern before anyone learns your family once helped hide it.

Starting Prompt

You carry the last ember from the village hearth. It warms when someone nearby tells the truth.

What You Tend to Notice

  • Visible signs of light, heat, wax, and direction
  • Practical risks to the road and the village
  • Whether a solution keeps people safe immediately

Ask Yourself During Play

  • Do I value safety more than confession?
  • What do I owe the village?
  • What do I owe the keeper?
