# Lanterns Below Marrow Hill — Storyboard

## Opening Summary
The road lantern at Marrow Hill fails at dusk. Fog covers the old road. Beneath the lantern post, a sealed stone stair opens for the first time in years. Two companions descend to recover the light.

---

## Scene 1 — The Stair Beneath the Lantern (12 min)

### Shared prompt
Read aloud:
> The village lantern dies at dusk, and a stone stair opens below it. The road beyond town is already filling with fog. Cold air rises from the dark below, carrying the smell of rain, stone, and extinguished wax. Whatever is hidden under Marrow Hill has waited a long time for someone to come down.

### Private prompt — The Wickbearer
> The ember you carry from the village hearth warms suddenly in your palm. It pulls you toward the dark as if recognizing the place. You sense the lantern can be restored, but the feeling comes with guilt. Your family knew more about this hill than anyone admitted.

### Private prompt — The Stone Listener
> As your hand touches the stair wall, the stone murmurs: “Light remembers debt.” The hill is not abandoned. It is listening. You know immediately that this place was sealed by choice, not by collapse or neglect.

### Scene action
- Each player says:
- one thing their character fears losing
- one reason their character agreed to come
- Move both tokens into **Entry Stair**, then **Candle Hall**.

### Unlock
Reveal:
- Lantern Clue A
- Lantern Clue B

### Success condition
Advance when both players have spoken and agreed to enter the shrine.

---

## Scene 2 — The Hall of Failing Candles (15 min)

### Shared prompt
Read aloud:
> The chamber ahead is lined with candles set into alcoves from floor to ceiling. Most burn low and blue, but three blaze brighter than the others, throwing long shadows that bend against the stone in different directions. The air is warm, but the room feels watchful. There are two clear ways forward: the carved arch to the Oath Room and the sloping passage toward the Root Vault.

### Private prompt — The Wickbearer
> The three bright candles form a shape your family taught you to recognize: a lantern sigil. It points not to the carved arch, but toward the lower path that leads to the Root Vault.

### Private prompt — The Stone Listener
> Between the alcoves, you find a vow carved into the wall: “We bind the willing light that the road may remember the way.” The next line is chipped, but the meaning is clear—this lantern was built to hold something, not merely to shine.

### Scene action
- Compare the two private prompts.
- Discuss:
- Do you investigate the **Oath Room** first?
- Or follow the sigil toward the **Root Vault**?
- No matter which is chosen first, the players ultimately uncover both sides of the truth.

### Unlock
Reveal:
- Seal Clue A
- Seal Clue B

### Success condition
Advance when the players can clearly answer:
1. Where is the lantern hidden?
2. Why was it hidden?

---

## Scene 3 — The Mirror Pool Oath (13 min)

### Shared prompt
Read aloud:
> A still pool lies in a round chamber of polished stone. Its surface reflects the room perfectly—until both of you step near it. Then the mirror changes. In the water, you appear older, tired, and carrying the hill lantern between you. Neither reflection looks afraid, but both look burdened.

### Private prompt — The Wickbearer
> Your reflection mouths silent words before sound reaches you: “A restored light still casts a prison shadow.” You understand that simply repairing the lantern does not erase what it costs.

### Private prompt — The Stone Listener
> The wall behind the pool whispers through the rock: “The keeper entered willingly, but no path was left for return.” The truth is no longer abstract. Someone accepted this duty, then was forgotten inside it.

### Scene action
- Each player asks the pool **one question**.
- The other player answers in-character as the reflection, using what they have learned so far.
- Good question examples:
- What will happen if the lantern is restored?
- What will happen if the keeper is freed?
- Is there another way?

### Unlock
Reveal:
- Key Clue A
- Key Clue B

### Success condition
Advance when both players understand that the final choice is moral, not mechanical.

---

## Scene 4 — The Lantern Heart (15 min)

### Shared prompt
Read aloud:
> The deepest chamber is threaded with living roots. Suspended between them hangs a brass lantern large enough to hold a person’s shadow. Inside glows a steady light, and within that light is the outline of a patient face. When you approach, a soft voice speaks from the glass: “The road still needs light. I still deserve a choice. What kind of mercy have you brought me?”

### Private prompt — The Wickbearer
> You know restoring the lantern will protect the village road tonight. You also know it means asking the keeper to remain where they are. If you choose safety, it will be immediate and visible.

### Private prompt — The Stone Listener
> You know freeing the keeper will end an old injustice, but the road may become dangerous again until people adapt. You also sense a third possibility: the flame might be shared.

### Scene action
- Each player gives **one sentence of testimony**:
- what they believe is right
- what they are willing to risk
- Make the final choice together:
- Restore the Lantern
- Free the Keeper
- Split the Flame

### Success condition
Read the matching ending aloud. Then each player gives a one-sentence epilogue for their character.

---

## Replay notes
The story is replayable because:
- the final decision can change
- players may emphasize safety, truth, or compromise differently
- role motivations shape the discussion and tone
