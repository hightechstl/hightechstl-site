Lanterns Below Marrow Hill — Ending Cards

Restore the Lantern

You lift the brass lantern together and speak the old words. Its flame grows steady, warm, and golden. Above, the road through the fog brightens in a single line all the way back to the village gate. A patient voice inside the glass thanks you for remembering your duty, though a trace of sadness remains in every note. By dawn, the villagers call you saviors. Yet on quiet nights, one of you still hears a whisper from the lantern, asking whether safety and mercy are always the same thing.

Epilogues

  • The Wickbearer is honored by the village and quietly decides whether to reveal their family’s part in the old sealing.
  • The Stone Listener leaves with the certainty that truth has been delayed, not erased.

Free the Keeper

You unfasten the latch and let the lantern open like a held breath. A figure made of pale light steps out, touches the hill wall, and smiles as if hearing dawn for the first time in years. The road above dims, and the fog thickens, but the keeper bows in gratitude before slipping into the night. When you return, the village has questions you cannot soften. The old bargain has ended. The danger is real. So is the freedom you chose.

Epilogues

  • The Wickbearer carries guilt for the risk, but also relief that a burden carried for generations has ended.
  • The Stone Listener becomes the first person to openly tell the true history of Marrow Hill.

Split the Flame

Instead of choosing between prison and darkness, you cup the flame together and divide it. Half returns to the lantern, where it burns lower but clean and calm. Half sinks into the roots beneath the shrine, spreading outward like glowing veins through the hill. Above, the road remains lit, though more softly than before. A second path opens below the roots, winding safely through the earth. The keeper’s voice changes from captive to companion. The village will keep its light, but it will also have to grow into the truth.

Epilogues

  • The Wickbearer becomes a keeper of the road light rather than a guardian of old secrets.
  • The Stone Listener guides the village along the hidden root path and teaches others what the hill remembers.
