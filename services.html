01

Managed IT Support

Ongoing technology support with clear ownership, useful documentation, and regular planning.

We resolve day-to-day issues, help your team use its tools effectively, and keep technology priorities from getting lost behind urgent work.

Who it helps

Businesses with 5 to 100 employees that need dependable support without hiring an internal IT department.

Business outcome

Less downtime, faster issue resolution, and a more predictable technology environment.

02

Network & WiFi Solutions

Reliable connectivity designed around your workspace, devices, staff, and customers.

We assess wired and wireless coverage, troubleshoot performance, plan upgrades, and coordinate providers so your network can support the way the business actually operates.

Who it helps

Offices, shops, and facilities with dead zones, slow connections, recurring outages, or unmanaged equipment.

Business outcome

Stable access, fewer disruptions, and better performance across business-critical systems.

03

Computer & Device Management

Practical lifecycle support for computers, printers, and connected business equipment.

We help standardize purchasing, set up devices, solve user issues, organize replacement planning, and reduce the hidden cost of inconsistent technology.

Who it helps

Teams managing a mix of aging computers, printers, shared workstations, and line-of-business devices.

Business outcome

Productive employees, fewer recurring problems, and smarter equipment investments.

04

Backup & Recovery Planning

A clear plan for protecting critical data and continuing through disruption.

We review what is backed up, identify gaps, coordinate solutions, and establish verification practices so backups are more than a checkbox.

Who it helps

Any business that depends on customer records, operational files, cloud applications, or local systems.

Business outcome

Reduced data-loss risk and a more confident, actionable recovery plan.

05

Technology Consulting

Experienced guidance for important decisions, purchases, and change.

We translate business goals into technology priorities, assess options, build practical roadmaps, and help leaders avoid costly decisions driven by vendor pressure or incomplete information.

Who it helps

Owners and leaders planning growth, replacing systems, opening locations, or evaluating a major technology purchase.

Business outcome

Clear priorities, controlled costs, and investments aligned with real business needs.

06

Fractional IT Management

Technology leadership and accountability without a full-time management hire.

We oversee priorities, vendors, documentation, continuity planning, and stakeholder communication as an extension of your leadership team.

Who it helps

Growing organizations with multiple vendors, increasing complexity, or no single owner for technology.

Business outcome

Better coordination, fewer surprises, and an IT strategy that keeps pace with the company.

07

Project Services

Focused expertise for upgrades, moves, deployments, and defined technology work.

We scope and lead projects, coordinate vendors, manage dependencies, and communicate progress so technology changes are completed with less disruption.

Who it helps

Businesses tackling an office move, network upgrade, equipment rollout, assessment, or other one-time initiative.

Business outcome

Well-planned execution, controlled risk, and a clear finish line.
